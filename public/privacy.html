Privacy Policy

Draft for review · Last updated 27 October 2026

This draft describes the current alpha and its known limitations. Nomigen is an unregistered project, not a registered company. The individual operator’s identity and contact address have not yet been published, and the backup deletion schedule has not been established. This is not a completed compliance notice.

Nomigen is an AI voice simulation platform. Oitu is its voice practice product, available through @oitubot on Telegram. This page explains how the project handles information for that service and this website. For privacy questions, contact founder@nomigen.com.

1. Information and purposes

Oitu processes your Telegram account and chat identifiers, the profile information Telegram makes available to the bot, your messages and voice recordings, transcriptions, conversation context, and generated replies and feedback. It uses this information to associate a session with you, run the scenario, provide contextual hints, and give feedback on your wording and vocal delivery.

Messages and audio can contain personal or sensitive details you choose to disclose. Use fictional details where possible. Do not submit confidential information, children’s data, or another person’s recordings or personal information without appropriate permission.

If you email the project, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. The website does not record your microphone or provide a form for submitting practice recordings. Visiting the website can still involve technical information, such as an IP address and browser information, being processed by hosting and delivery services to serve the pages.

This draft does not yet establish the operator’s applicable legal jurisdiction or a complete account of the legal bases and international transfer arrangements for processing. These matters require review before the notice is finalized.

2. Service providers

Providing voice practice involves sending relevant information to external services:

  • Telegram delivers your interactions with the bot and processes chat data through its own platform. See the Telegram Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs receives audio for speech recognition and text for speech synthesis. Its handling of API data depends on the applicable service terms, contract and account settings. See the ElevenLabs Privacy Policy and the training information below.
  • OpenRouter and model providers process relevant conversation content to classify inputs and generate responses. Current models include the Jav classifier, DeepSeek and Gemini, accessed through OpenRouter. OpenRouter routes requests to model providers; a model name is not necessarily the name of the hosting provider. See the OpenRouter Privacy Policy for information about routing and provider practices.
  • Play2Go supplies the rented servers hosting the bot and its database. See the Play2Go Privacy Policy.

Providers may process data in countries other than your own. This draft does not specify server locations or promise that processing stays in a particular country. Provider retention and access rules are separate from the project’s own storage practices; where applicable, service contracts govern processing on the project’s behalf.

3. Model training settings

The project has disabled model training use in its OpenRouter account settings. This does not mean that every provider has zero retention, or that no processing occurs outside the project.

ElevenLabs currently uses its standard account settings for the project; no training opt-out has been confirmed. Depending on the account and applicable terms, data submitted to ElevenLabs may be used to improve its models. The project therefore does not promise that all submitted data is excluded from provider training. See ElevenLabs’ explanation of model improvement and opt-out settings.

4. Storage and deletion

There is currently no automatic expiry for stored conversations. Messages, audio and associated practice data are retained indefinitely unless you request deletion through the bot.

Use the bot’s data deletion function to request removal of your saved service data. Deletion from the project’s active storage, including messages and audio, takes place within one hour. If you cannot find or use that function, contact founder@nomigen.com for help; the one-hour timeframe describes the bot function, not an email support response time.

Backup copies may remain after active data is deleted. A backup expiry period and deletion procedure have not yet been established, so the project cannot give a date for removal of those copies.

The bot’s deletion function does not guarantee deletion from Telegram or other providers’ systems, nor does it reverse any processing already performed by a provider. Their own retention rules and deletion processes may apply. You can separately manage your Telegram chat through Telegram’s controls.

5. Access and security

The project does not routinely review practice conversations by hand. This does not mean that the operator or service providers are technically unable to access stored data, or that provider personnel never have access under their own terms.

No online service can promise absolute confidentiality or security. This alpha should not be used as a store for secrets or highly sensitive records. It does not promise end-to-end encryption across the Telegram, transcription and AI processing workflow.

6. Planned Google Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is planned but is not connected to this version of the website. No Google Analytics tracking script is included, and the website currently sets no application cookies of its own.

If enabled later, Google Analytics would be used to understand visits and website usage. Depending on its configuration, it can collect page interactions and device information and use cookies such as _ga to distinguish visitors and sessions. See Google’s explanation of Analytics cookies and the Google Privacy Policy.

The configuration, retention settings and any required consent controls need to be established before analytics is enabled. This draft does not treat a visit to the website as consent to future tracking.

7. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the bot and request deletion as described above. Depending on the law applicable to you and the operator, you may also have rights to access or correct personal information, obtain a copy, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a relevant data protection authority.

Send requests to founder@nomigen.com. Include enough information to identify the relevant Telegram account, but do not email passwords or unnecessary identity documents. The project may need to verify that a request concerns your own data.

Oitu is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 or over. Do not use the service or submit recordings if you are under 18. Please contact the project if you believe a child’s data has been submitted.

8. Updates and contact

This page will be revised as the alpha and its data practices change. The revision date appears at the top. The draft label remains until the outstanding operator information, retention arrangements and other legal details have been reviewed.

Contact: founder@nomigen.com. Read the Terms of Use draft for the service’s intended use and limitations.
