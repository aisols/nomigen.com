1. The alpha service

Oitu offers asynchronous voice practice through @oitubot on Telegram. You exchange voice notes with synthetic AI characters, can request contextual hints, and receive generated feedback on wording and vocal delivery.

The current alpha is free and open to eligible adults. There is no subscription or payment requirement for current alpha access. Any future paid offering would need separate pricing and terms before purchase.

Live calls, real-time interruption and other roadmap features are not part of the current promise of service. Website reports and scores marked as illustrative are examples, not validated assessments of you.

2. Adults only

You must be at least 18 years old to use Oitu. The service is designed exclusively for adult learners. Do not submit messages or recordings from or about children.

You are responsible for access to your Telegram account and for following Telegram’s Terms of Service when using its platform.

3. Practice, not professional care

Oitu is a rehearsal tool. It is not a substitute for clinical therapy, medical advice or other professional advice. It does not diagnose, treat or assess a medical condition, and it does not guarantee confidence, relationship, negotiation or workplace outcomes.

AI characters simulate emotional delivery; they do not have genuine feelings or knowledge of your real circumstances. Replies and feedback can be inaccurate, incomplete, culturally inappropriate or unsuitable for your situation. Use your own judgment before applying an example in real life.

Scenes may involve disagreement, refusal or pressure. You can stop a practice session whenever you wish. Oitu is not an emergency or crisis service, and conversations are not routinely monitored by a person. If you need immediate help, contact the appropriate local emergency service or a qualified professional.

4. Responsible use

Use the service for lawful, respectful practice. In particular:

  • Submit only content you are entitled to use. Prefer your own voice and fictional scenario details; obtain appropriate permission before sharing another adult’s personal information or recordings.
  • Do not use the service to impersonate a real person, clone someone’s voice without authorization, deceive others, or violate privacy or intellectual property rights.
  • Do not use generated material to threaten, harass, exploit or manipulate other people. Practice scenarios are not permission to carry out harmful behavior.
  • Do not attempt unauthorized access, disrupt the service, distribute malicious content or bypass access restrictions.

The project may restrict or suspend access to address misuse, protect the service or comply with applicable requirements. Contact the project if you believe a restriction was applied in error.

This draft proposes no broad license to sell or publicly publish your conversations. 6. Storage and leaving the service

You can stop using Oitu at any time. Stopping use does not automatically erase saved conversations: messages and audio currently have no automatic expiry.

The bot’s data deletion function removes your saved data from the project’s active storage within one hour. Copies may remain in backups, whose cleanup schedule is not yet established. This function does not guarantee removal from Telegram or other provider systems. See Storage and deletion for the full limits and contact the project if you need help.

The service depends on Telegram, hosting and AI providers, which can also experience interruptions or change their services. Nomigen may pause or discontinue the alpha. Roadmap descriptions and grant applications are not commitments to delivery dates or evidence of a grant award.
